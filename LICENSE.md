## Educational models

SIDUS calculators are **educational pure-SI models**. They are **not**
flight-certified ephemerides, ECLSS design authority, or medical/flight rules.

Third-party names (NASA, ESA, ISS, Dragon, …) appear only for educational
context and source citation. SIDUS is an independent open-source project.
